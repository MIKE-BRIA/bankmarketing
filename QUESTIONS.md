# 📋 Business Analysis Brief — Bank Marketing Campaign

## Background

The marketing team at a Portuguese banking institution has conducted a series
of telemarketing campaigns aimed at getting customers to subscribe to a term
deposit product. The campaigns involved direct phone calls to customers, and
in many cases multiple calls were made to the same customer.

The team has collected data from these campaigns and needs answers to the
following business questions to help improve future campaign performance,
reduce wasted resources, and better understand their customer base.

---

## Business Questions

1. What is the overall subscription rate from this campaign — how many
   customers said yes versus no?

2. What is the average age of the customers we are reaching, and does age
   play a role in whether someone subscribes?

3. Which job types make up the majority of our customer base, and which
   job types are most likely to subscribe to the term deposit?

4. Does a customer's education level influence their decision to subscribe?

5. Are married, single, or divorced customers more likely to respond
   positively to our campaign?

6. How does a customer's average account balance relate to their likelihood
   of subscribing — are wealthier customers more likely to say yes?

7. Are customers who already have a housing loan less likely to subscribe
   to a new financial product?

8. Does having an existing personal loan affect a customer's willingness
   to subscribe?

9. Which contact method — cellular or telephone — produces better
   subscription outcomes?

10. Which months of the year yield the highest subscription rates, and are
    there months where the campaign performs poorly?

11. What day of the week tends to produce the best response from customers?

12. How long does a typical call last, and is there a relationship between
    call duration and whether a customer subscribes?

13. How many times are we contacting each customer on average, and does
    contacting someone more times increase or decrease the chance of
    a subscription?

14. Are customers who were contacted in a previous campaign more likely to
    subscribe in the current one?

15. What was the outcome of our previous campaigns, and does a past
    successful outcome predict future success with the same customer?

16. Are there customers in our database with unusually high or low account
    balances that may be skewing our financial analysis?

17. If we segment our customers into four groups based on account balance,
    what does each group look like and how do their subscription rates
    compare?

18. What does the profile of a customer who subscribes look like — what
    combination of age, job, balance, and education is most common among
    subscribers?

19. Are there any customer segments we are spending significant resources
    contacting that consistently do not convert — and should we stop
    targeting them?

20. If the team could only contact a limited number of customers in the
    next campaign, which segment should they prioritize to maximize
    subscription rates?

21. Based on everything in the data, what are the top three changes the
    marketing team should make to improve the performance of future
    campaigns?

---

## Deliverables

The analyst is expected to work through each question above, provide
supporting data and visualizations, and conclude with a summary of
findings and actionable recommendations for the marketing team.

All analysis will be documented in the project notebook and summarized
in the final report.
